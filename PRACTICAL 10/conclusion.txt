Conclusion of Kruskal's Algorithm
Kruskal's Algorithm is a greedy algorithm used to find the Minimum Spanning Tree (MST)
 of a connected, weighted, undirected graph. It selects edges in increasing order of their 
 weights without forming any cycles. It connects all vertices with the minimum total cost. 
 Its time complexity is O(E log E), making it efficient for finding the Minimum Spanning Tree.
