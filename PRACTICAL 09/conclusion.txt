Conclusion
Prim’s Algorithm is used to find the Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) of a connected, weighted, undirected graph.
 It repeatedly selects the minimum-weight edge to connect an unvisited vertex to the tree. Using a min-heap
  improves efficiency.
- Time Complexity: O(E\log E)
- Space Complexity: O(V+E)
