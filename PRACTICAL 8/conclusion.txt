BFS (Breadth First Search) visits the vertices level by level using a queue (FIFO), while DFS (Depth First Search) explores
 as deep as possible using a stack (LIFO). Both algorithms have a time complexity of O(V + E) and space complexity of O(V). 
 BFS is useful for finding the shortest path in an unweighted graph, whereas DFS is useful for deep traversal and exploring
  connected components.
